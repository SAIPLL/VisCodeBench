Each .pt file in "data/" contains samples in the form  {sample_id: sample_data}.
The sample data is dict as well, and the key `feat` in the sample_data is a 2D vector that represents two feature dimension of the pre-computed TSNE.
the key `view` is a zero-one value that indicates whether the sample is real or generated.
