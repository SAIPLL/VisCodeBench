For the given "Parameter.xlsx", this code generates a comprehensive visualization to analyze the impact of the beta parameter on model performance across three datasets (ECAD, SMEsD, and DBLP).
The visualization consists of three subplots, one for each dataset, displaying the variation of three key performance metrics (Accuracy, F1, and AUC) as beta changes.
Each metric is represented by a distinct line style and color (green for AUC, blue for F1, and a viridis-derived color for Accuracy) with unique markers (triangle, square, and circle).
The y-axis ranges are customized for each dataset to better highlight the performance variations.
The plots feature a grid layout, Times New Roman font, and are saved as a high-resolution image (800 DPI) named 'PA.jpg'.
The legend is strategically placed at the bottom of the last subplot for better readability.
