The file data_dicts.json has all the data necessary for the following visualizations.


On the first level, there are keys for the two models, 'Mistral' and 'Qwen'.

For each of the models, there are keys called 'data_', 'bracket_boxplots', and 'brackets'.


The final figure consists of four panels, organized in a 2x2 grid.

The two panels in the top row (called a and b) visualize results for the Mistral model and the two panels in the bottom row (c and d) visualize results for the Qwen model.

Panels a and c show boxplots of the two data series stored with the key 'data_' for the respective models - the first series is captioned 'pers.', the second series is captioned 'no pers.'.

Panels b and d also show boxplots.

The data for panels b and d is primarily coming from the key 'bracket_boxplots' for the respective models.

In order of their appearance in the dictionary, for each of the 90 keys in 'bracket_boxplots' a boxplot is generated based on the series contained in the key's corresponding value.

At the same horizontal locations as for the 90 keys, a red circle marker is added for each of the 90 values stored in the respective 'brackets' keys.

The resulting 90 boxplots are vertically separated by two dotted lines both in panels b and d drawn after 30 and after 60 boxplots.

Beneath panel d, the thereby resulting three sections are captioned '30 min personas', '30 median personas' and '30 max personas'.
