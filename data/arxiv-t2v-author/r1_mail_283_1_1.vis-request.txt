The chart shows the recommendation performance on a Book dataset by varying the methods used for calculating the matching score.

The methods compared are BERT, BLUE, ROUGE, and TaxRec.

The performance is measured using three metrics: Recall@1, Recall@5, and Recall@10, represented by distinct bars for each matching method.

1.Recall@1 (light green bars): Performance is consistently low across all methods but shows an improvement from BERT to TaxRec.

2.Recall@5 (peach bars): Performance increases progressively from BERT to TaxRec, indicating TaxRec has a significant advantage.

3.Recall@10 (lavender bars): This metric has the highest values for all methods, with TaxRec achieving the best score, followed by ROUGE, BLUE, and BERT.
 
The TaxRec method demonstrates the best overall performance across all metrics, suggesting it is more effective at generating accurate recommendations for this dataset.
