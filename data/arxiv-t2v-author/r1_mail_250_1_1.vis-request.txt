Plot six heatmaps.
Each heatmap shall have the perturbation level on the y-axis and the group of music entities (FMT Passed, FMT Failed, Post-Cutoff) on the x-axis.
The numbers in the cells shall be the proportion of items for a given perturbation level and music entity group, which are associated with an error.
In total there are six errors (WoA spurious, WoA incorrect, WoA missed, Artist spurious, Artist incorrect, Artist missed) and for each you shall produce exactly one heatmap.
