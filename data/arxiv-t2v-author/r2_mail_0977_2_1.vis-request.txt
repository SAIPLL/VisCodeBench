For the given data, please create a scatter plot with a figure size of (4, 2.7).
The x-axis represents the "GFLOPs" column in the "Cell_flops.csv" file.
The y-axis shows the "mean" Dice Scores in the "Cell_each_dsc.csv" file, with standard errors in the "std" column.
 The series in the two files are the same, so you can merge them into one.
However, merge the mean and standard values into one dataframe with the FLOPs data, and then merge the values of each fold with the computation data.
Therefore, we have two dataframes.
The scatter plot will draw mean and standard values only, but the Dice Scores per seed are used for setting the scale of the y-axis.
The markers for mean are "*" and for standard errors are ".", while the color map is "tab10".
After drawing the values, create a dashed horizontal line corresponding to the mean of the "Ours" series.
 From this dashed line, draw vertical dashed lines to the mean values of the other series.
The y-axis shows ticks every 5 units with a label of "Dice (%, $\uparrow$)", but the x-axis labeled "FLOPs (G, $\downarrow$)" shows ticks every 20 units.
Use a tight layout and the "darkgrid" theme.
