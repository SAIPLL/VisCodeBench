The top left visualization should show the "Unigram Frequency Distribution" and be a line chart with all of the distributions in "uni_hist.txt" in color and the distribution in "all_uni_perc.txt" in black.

The y-axis for the top visualizations is "% of word frequency".

The bottom visualization should show the "Sentence Length Distribution" and be a line chart with all of the distributions in "sent_hist.txt" in color and the distribution in "all_hist_perc.txt" in black.
